Wrap Shell
Cottage Cheese Wrap
2-ingredient high-protein wrap shell. Bake on parchment — use with any filling. The foundation recipe.
155Cal
20gProtein
6gCarbs
5gFat

Ingredients (makes 2 wraps)

  • 1 cup cottage cheese (2% or full-fat)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp onion powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, eggs, and seasonings for 1-2 minutes until completely smooth.
  3. Pour onto parchment and spread evenly into a thin rectangle.
  4. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden at edges and set through.
  5. Cool completely — it firms up as it cools. Cut in half and fill.
Add 2 tbsp parmesan for a sturdier wrap. Change seasoning to match filling (Italian, Mexican, Indian). Stores 3-4 days in fridge.

Moong Dal Chilla
Indian lentil crepe stuffed with paneer and vegetables. High fiber, gut-health friendly. Also works as a wrap shell.
224Cal
12gProtein
24gCarbs
5gFiber

Batter (makes 7 chillas)

  • 1 cup moong dal (husked green gram), soaked 2 hours
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ inch ginger
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tsp salt, ½ cup water

Stuffing

  • 200g paneer, grated
  • ¼ cup onions, ½ cup spring onion greens
  • ½ cup each: carrots, red bell pepper, green bell pepper
  • 2 green chillies, ½ cup coriander
  • 1 tsp each: cumin powder, amchur, salt

Method

  1. Grind soaked dal with cumin, ginger, chillies, salt until smooth. Add water for thick batter.
  2. Mix all stuffing ingredients.
  3. Pour batter on hot non-stick tawa, spread into a round.
  4. Drizzle ghee. Cook 30-40 seconds until set. Add stuffing, press gently.
  5. Flip, cook 1-2 minutes until crisp. Fold and serve.

Wrap
Buffalo Chicken Wraps
Rotisserie chicken, Greek yogurt ranch, buffalo sauce, coleslaw. No cooking, 20 minutes. Absolute banger.
210Cal
20gProtein
20gCarbs
3gFiber

Ingredients (makes 10 wraps)

  • 450g rotisserie chicken, pulled
  • 225g coleslaw mix
  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup buffalo/hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp ranch seasoning
  • 85g blue cheese or cheddar
  • 225g baby spinach and arugula
  • 10 cottage cheese wraps or low-carb tortillas

Method

  1. Combine Greek yogurt, ranch seasoning, and buffalo sauce in a large bowl.
  2. Add pulled chicken, coleslaw mix, and cheese. Toss until coated.
  3. Layer greens, chicken mixture, more greens on each wrap.
  4. Roll tightly. Slice in half.
Greens between filling and wrap prevent sogginess. For extra heat, add more buffalo sauce.

Peri Peri Chicken Wrap
Charred spicy chicken with smoky paprika mayo. Nando's vibes at a fraction of the calories.
376Cal
50gProtein
28gCarbs
8gFat

Chicken (per wrap)

  • 150g chicken breast, sliced thin
  • ½ tsp each: smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, chilli flakes, oregano, basil, salt, pepper
  • ½ tsp coconut oil

Peri Peri Sauce

  • 2 tbsp light mayo
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp each: smoked paprika, cayenne, garlic powder

Method

  1. Coat chicken in all seasoning.
  2. Cook on high heat 7-8 minutes until charred and done.
  3. Mix sauce ingredients. Spread on wrap.
  4. Add lettuce, red onion, tomato, chicken, remaining sauce. Roll.
Use cottage cheese wrap to drop carbs. Toast seam-side down for crispy finish.

Sauce

  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced

Assembly (serves 4)

Chicken Tikka Wraps
Tandoori-marinated chicken with Kashmiri chilli, amchur, yogurt. Worth the overnight marinade.
527Cal
41gProtein
48gCarbs
12gFiber

Marinade (makes 6 wraps)

  • ½ cup Greek yogurt (0% fat)
  • 1 lemon juice, 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, 2-inch ginger (chopped)
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp Kashmiri chilli
  • 1 tsp amchur, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp aniseed
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 900g chicken breast, cut into 24 pieces

Method

  1. Whisk marinade. Coat chicken. Refrigerate overnight (2 days ideal).
  2. Thread 4 pieces per skewer. Broil 10 min, flip, 10 min more.
  3. Spread tomato sauce on wrap. Add greens, chicken, raita or avocado dip.
  4. Roll tightly.
Use cottage cheese wrap for low carb. The marinade is everything — don't rush it.

Chicken Meatballs & Zoodles
Herb-loaded chicken meatballs in marinara over spiralised courgette. Classic comfort, fraction of the calories.
325Cal
25gProtein
25gCarbs
7gFiber

Meatballs (serves 4, ~16 balls)

  • 600g lean chicken mince
  • 1 cup grated zucchini (squeeze out liquid)
  • 2 slices bread + ¼ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, garlic, onion
  • Fresh oregano, parsley, mint

Method

  1. Mix all meatball ingredients. Form 16 balls. Chill 15 min.
  2. Pan-fry in olive oil over low-medium heat, ~15-20 min.
  3. Spiralise 4 zucchini. Sauté with garlic 1-2 min max.
  4. Serve with meatballs, warm marinara, parmesan.
Grated zucchini in the meatballs keeps them incredibly moist. Can grill instead.

Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta
Cottage cheese cream sauce, cajun spice, bell peppers, chickpea pasta. Creamy without the guilt.
350Cal
38gProtein
25gCarbs
10gFat

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 112g chickpea or protein pasta
  • 400g chicken breast, diced
  • Cajun seasoning, thyme, paprika, cayenne
  • 1 red bell pepper, spring onions

Cottage Cheese Cream Sauce

  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese (2%)
  • ½ cup 2% milk
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp cajun seasoning

Method

  1. Cook pasta. Reserve 1 cup water.
  2. Season and cook chicken. Set aside.
  3. Sauté veg. Deglaze with pasta water.
  4. Blend sauce ingredients until smooth. Add to pan on low heat.
  5. Toss in chicken and pasta. Don't boil the sauce.
Use zucchini noodles instead of pasta for even lower carbs. The cottage cheese sauce is the secret.

Thai Basil Ground Chicken
Garlic, shallot, serrano chilli, fish sauce, oyster sauce, basil. Thai street food flavour, 20 minutes.
190Cal
27gProtein
8gCarbs
7gFat

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 450g lean ground chicken
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, 1 shallot, 1 serrano pepper (all minced)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • Large handful fresh basil

Method

  1. Make paste from shallot, serrano, garlic. Fry 2-3 min.
  2. Add chicken, break apart, cook through.
  3. Mix sauces. Pour over chicken, stir 30-60 sec.

227Cal

13gFat

Crust (serves 4)

  • 1 cup cottage cheese, 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp parmesan, 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt & pepper

Toppings

  • ½ cup marinara, ¾ cup mozzarella
  • ½ cup chicken pepperoni or grilled chicken

Method

Chipotle BBQ Stuffed Sweet Potato
Ground chicken in smoky BBQ-chipotle sauce over roasted sweet potato with spinach.
260Cal
21gProtein
33gCarbs
5gFiber

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 3 large sweet potatoes, halved
  • 450g ground chicken
  • ½ cup BBQ sauce, 2 chipotle peppers in adobo
  • 4 cloves garlic, ½ cup stock
  • 85g baby spinach
  • Greek yogurt, coriander, pickled onions

Method

  1. Roast sweet potatoes cut-side down at 230°C (450°F), 40-50 min.
  2. Blend BBQ sauce, chipotle, garlic, stock until smooth.
  3. Brown chicken. Add sauce. Fold in spinach.
  4. Top sweet potatoes with chicken mixture and garnishes.
The chipotle-BBQ sauce is smoky and addictive. Batch-prep the filling for the week.
