Cottage Cheese Wrap
2-ingredient high-protein wrap shell. Bake on parchment — use with any filling. The foundation recipe.
155Cal
20gProtein
6gCarbs
5gFat

Ingredients (serves 2)

  • 1 cup cottage cheese (2% or full-fat)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp onion powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, eggs, and seasonings for 1-2 minutes until completely smooth.
  3. Pour onto parchment and spread evenly into a thin rectangle.
  4. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden at edges and set through.
  5. Cool completely — it firms up as it cools. Cut in half and fill.
Add 2 tbsp parmesan for a sturdier wrap. Change seasoning to match filling (Italian, Mexican, Indian). Stores 3-4 days in fridge.
