Wrap Shell
Cottage Cheese Wrap
2-ingredient high-protein wrap shell. Bake on parchment — use with any filling. The foundation recipe.
155Cal
20gProtein
6gCarbs
5gFat

Ingredients (makes 2 wraps)

  • 1 cup cottage cheese (2% or full-fat)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp onion powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, eggs, and seasonings for 1-2 minutes until completely smooth.
  3. Pour onto parchment and spread evenly into a thin rectangle.
  4. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden at edges and set through.
  5. Cool completely — it firms up as it cools. Cut in half and fill.
Add 2 tbsp parmesan for a sturdier wrap. Change seasoning to match filling (Italian, Mexican, Indian). Stores 3-4 days in fridge.

Moong Dal Chilla
Indian lentil crepe stuffed with paneer and vegetables. High fiber, gut-health friendly. Also works as a wrap shell.
224Cal
12gProtein
24gCarbs
5gFiber

Batter (makes 7 chillas)

  • 1 cup moong dal (husked green gram), soaked 2 hours
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ inch ginger
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tsp salt, ½ cup water

Stuffing

  • 200g paneer, grated
  • ¼ cup onions, ½ cup spring onion greens
  • ½ cup each: carrots, red bell pepper, green bell pepper
  • 2 green chillies, ½ cup coriander
  • 1 tsp each: cumin powder, amchur, salt

Method

  1. Grind soaked dal with cumin, ginger, chillies, salt until smooth. Add water for thick batter.
  2. Mix all stuffing ingredients.
  3. Pour batter on hot non-stick tawa, spread into a round.
  4. Drizzle ghee. Cook 30-40 seconds until set. Add stuffing, press gently.
  5. Flip, cook 1-2 minutes until crisp. Fold and serve.

Buffalo Chicken Wraps
Rotisserie chicken, Greek yogurt ranch, buffalo sauce, coleslaw. No cooking, 20 minutes. Absolute banger.
210Cal
20gProtein
20gCarbs
3gFiber

Ingredients (makes 10 wraps)

  • 450g rotisserie chicken, pulled
  • 225g coleslaw mix
  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup buffalo/hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp ranch seasoning
  • 85g blue cheese or cheddar
  • 225g baby spinach and arugula
  • 10 cottage cheese wraps or low-carb tortillas

Method

  1. Combine Greek yogurt, ranch seasoning, and buffalo sauce in a large bowl.
  2. Add pulled chicken, coleslaw mix, and cheese. Toss until coated.
  3. Layer greens, chicken mixture, more greens on each wrap.
  4. Roll tightly. Slice in half.
Greens between filling and wrap prevent sogginess. For extra heat, add more buffalo sauce.

Peri Peri Chicken Wrap
Charred spicy chicken with smoky paprika mayo. Nando's vibes at a fraction of the calories.

Chicken (per wrap)

Tandoori-marinated chicken with Kashmiri chilli, amchur, yogurt. Worth the overnight marinade.
527Cal
41gProtein
48gCarbs
12gFiber

Marinade (makes 6 wraps)

  • 1 lemon juice, 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, 2-inch ginger (chopped)

  • 600g lean chicken mince

Cauliflower Rice Chicken Biryani
Proper biryani flavours — garam masala, Kashmiri chilli, saffron, mint — with cauliflower rice keeping carbs at 17g. Dev's pick.
382Cal
42gProtein
17gCarbs
6gFiber

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 680g chicken breast or thighs, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp ginger, 1 tsp garlic
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder, ½ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ cup fresh mint, ¼ cup coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp yogurt, 1½ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup ghee
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp shah jeera (or cumin seeds), 1 bay leaf
  • 6 cups riced cauliflower
  • Pinch saffron + 1 tbsp warm milk (optional)

Method

  1. Combine spices, herbs, lemon juice, yogurt and salt. Marinate chicken 20 min (overnight ideal).
  2. Process cauliflower florets in food processor until riced.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp ghee. Sauté onions 10-15 min until golden and caramelised. Reserve half.
  4. Add remaining ghee, cumin seeds, bay leaf. Sauté briefly. Add marinated chicken, cook covered 5-7 min.
  5. Add cauliflower rice and salt. Combine gently. Cook covered on low-medium 10 min.
  6. Top with reserved onions, saffron milk, mint and coriander.
The caramelised onions and saffron make this taste like proper biryani. Cauliflower rice absorbs all the spice.

Chicken Keema with Peas
Classic Indian mince curry — cumin, coriander, garam masala, tomato. 40g protein, 12g carbs. Serve over cauliflower rice. Dev's pick.
260Cal
40gProtein
12gCarbs
3gFiber

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 2 tsp oil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 small onion (80g), chopped
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garam masala
  • ¾ tsp salt, ½ tsp turmeric, ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli (optional)
  • 450g lean chicken mince
  • 225g tomato sauce
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • Fresh coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil over medium-high. Add bay leaf, cook 30 seconds.
  2. Add onion and chilli, cook 5 min until browned.
  3. Stir in ginger and garlic, cook 2 min.
  4. Add chicken mince and all spices. Cook 5 min until browned, breaking up meat.
  5. Add tomato sauce plus one can of water. Simmer 7-8 min until liquid mostly absorbs.
  6. Stir in peas and coriander. Cook until heated through.
Serve over cauliflower rice for low-carb, or regular basmati. The tomato base keeps it moist without adding cream.

High-Protein Butter Chicken
Greek yogurt marinade, silken tofu blended into sauce instead of cream. 61g protein per serving. Dea's pick.
490Cal
61gProtein
37gCarbs
10gFat

Chicken Marinade (serves 4)

  • 900g chicken breast, bite-sized
  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 4-5 cloves garlic, grated
  • 2-inch knob ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp salt

Sauce

  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4-5 cloves garlic, grated + 2-inch ginger
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can (425g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
  • ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp kasuri methi
  • 1 pack (300g) firm silken tofu

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt mixture at least 30 min.
  2. Sear marinated chicken in batches until browned.
  3. Cook onion until golden. Add garlic, ginger, tomato paste until fragrant.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes and spices. Simmer 10-15 min.
  5. Blend silken tofu smooth. Combine with sauce and blend until silky. Return to pan.
  6. Add chicken, simmer until cooked through. Serve with ½ cup basmati rice and coriander.
The silken tofu trick makes the sauce creamy without cream or coconut milk. Macros include ½ cup basmati rice.

Chicken Alfredo Lasagna
Cottage cheese alfredo sauce, chicken, mozzarella, parmesan. 500 cal, 43g protein. Dea's pick.
500Cal
43gProtein
31gCarbs
24gFat

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 450g chicken breast, cooked and chopped
  • 2 cups (450g) low-fat cottage cheese
  • ¾ cup (180g) alfredo sauce
  • 225g fresh mozzarella, shredded
  • 115g parmesan, grated
  • 9 sheets no-boil lasagna
  • 3-4 handfuls fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Cook or air-fry chicken, chop into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Blend cottage cheese, alfredo sauce, salt and pepper until smooth.
  4. Thin sauce layer in baking dish. Layer: 3 lasagna sheets, sauce, chicken, parsley, mozzarella, parmesan. Repeat, finishing with cheese.
  5. Cover and bake 30 min. Uncover, bake 10-15 min until cheese bubbles and browns.
  6. Rest 5 min before serving. Garnish with parsley and parmesan.
The cottage cheese alfredo is indistinguishable from the real thing once blended. Leftovers reheat well.

Chicken Ramen Bowls
Red curry-miso broth, coconut milk, rotisserie chicken, rice ramen. Warming, rich, 38g protein. Dea's pick.
445Cal
38gProtein
45gCarbs
12gFat

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 450g rotisserie or cooked chicken, chopped
  • ¼ cup teriyaki or Japanese BBQ sauce
  • 170g ramen noodles (2 blocks)

Broth

  • 1 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp powdered peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp coconut aminos or low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 can (400ml) light coconut milk
  • 1½ cups chicken bone broth

Method

  1. Boil water for ramen.
  2. Mix curry paste, miso, honey, peanut butter, coconut aminos. Set aside.
  3. Heat chicken with BBQ sauce 3-5 min until caramelised. Set aside.
  4. Add curry paste mixture to pan, cook 20-30 sec. Add broth and coconut milk. Simmer.
  5. Cook noodles. Add to simmering broth.
  6. Serve: noodles, broth, chicken. Top with cucumber, spring onions, sesame, chilli crisp.
The miso-curry-peanut broth is deeply flavourful. Use rotisserie chicken for zero-effort protein.
