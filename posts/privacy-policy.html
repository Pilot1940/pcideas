Privacy Policy

How this site handles your information — and what it collects in the first place.

Effective April 25, 2026  ·  Governing law: Canada (PIPEDA)

Last updated: April 25, 2026
Short version: This is a personal blog. It collects no personal information directly. The only data collection happens through Google Analytics, which records standard visit statistics. No data is sold, no accounts exist, no forms collect personal details.

1. Who operates this site

This website (pcideas.chitalkar.com) is operated by Parikshit Chitalkar, an individual based in Canada. Questions or requests regarding this privacy policy can be directed to pilot@chitalkar.com.

2. Applicable law

This policy is governed by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), S.C. 2000, c. 5, Canada's federal private-sector privacy legislation. PIPEDA establishes ten fair information principles — accountability, identifying purposes, consent, limiting collection, limiting use/disclosure/retention, accuracy, safeguards, openness, individual access, and challenging compliance — which this site follows.

3. What information is collected

This site does not operate user accounts, comment systems, mailing lists, or contact forms. No personal information is submitted to or stored by this site directly.

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (property ID: G-VNP6QSWCCZ), a web analytics service operated by Google LLC. When you visit a page, Google Analytics may collect:

  • Your approximate geographic location (country, region, city — derived from IP address)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device type
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website or source
  • Session and interaction data (clicks, scroll depth)

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to distinguish visitors and aggregate usage patterns. Your IP address is used to derive location but is not stored in identifiable form by Google Analytics under its default configuration.

4. Cookies

This site sets cookies only through Google Analytics. These are first-party analytics cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_<ID>, which persist for up to 2 years to distinguish unique visitors. No advertising, tracking, or retargeting cookies are set by this site.

You can opt out of Google Analytics measurement by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

5. Purpose of collection

Analytics data is collected solely to understand how content is read — which articles attract readers, which topics are popular, and how visitors find the site. This information is used only to guide editorial decisions. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

6. Third-party data processors

The only third party that receives data in connection with visits to this site is Google LLC, through Google Analytics 4. Google's data practices are governed by its own Privacy Policy and its role as a data processor under applicable agreements. This site does not share visit data with any other third party.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft subsidiary). GitHub may log server-side request metadata (IP addresses, request timestamps, paths) as part of its standard infrastructure operations. GitHub's privacy practices are described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months under the site's current Analytics configuration, after which it is automatically deleted. No other personal data is retained by this site, as none is collected.

8. Your rights under PIPEDA

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access — request information about what personal data, if any, is held about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw consent to collection or use of your personal information
  • Complaint — file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your privacy rights have been violated

Because this site collects no personal data directly, requests for access or correction may be more appropriately directed to Google regarding Analytics data held under your browser's cookie identifiers.

To exercise any of these rights, or to raise a privacy concern, contact: pilot@chitalkar.com.

Complaints may also be directed to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact
